# Plans Command
List out the plans we have in ./plans/*

## Purpose
To help decide what to work on next.

## Steps

1. **Initial Listing**
   - List out a super short numbered list of all plans. So that I can read them quickly and ask you to expand them.

2. **After listing out plans**
    - After listing the plans, wait for me to respond about what I want to do and then act accordingly.

## Success Criteria
- ✅ Plans listed out
- ✅ Addressed anything I asked about
